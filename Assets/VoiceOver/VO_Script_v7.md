# Journey of Light · VO script v7

25 October 2026. Every line the visitor hears, in the order they hear it. How the moments work, why the lines say what they say, science checks and what to re-record are in `VO_Notes.md`.

**Voice.** Female. Calm, close, second person. Wonder, not lecture. About 140 words a minute.

**How to read it.** Each row is one recording. **ID** is the line and its file name. **When** is the moment it plays. **≈** is the read time. IDs starting with `N_` are button prompts: they play only if the visitor needs help, and only once.
